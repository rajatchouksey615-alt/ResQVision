# Team Code of Conduct

ResQVision is an academic collaboration. Team members and contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat teammates and reviewers with respect and patience.
- Discuss ideas and code directly, using evidence and clear reasoning.
- Give actionable feedback about the work rather than personal judgments.
- Make space for different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Respect privacy, project data restrictions, and agreed ownership.
- Raise concerns early and use the team’s agreed communication and escalation channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person’s private information, and misuse or unauthorized sharing of project data are not acceptable.

## Reporting and Response

Report concerns privately to the project supervisor or the team contact agreed by the project team. Share only the information needed to address the concern. The team should handle reports promptly, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality. A person involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker for its resolution.

This document sets collaboration expectations for the project and is not a substitute for institutional policies.
