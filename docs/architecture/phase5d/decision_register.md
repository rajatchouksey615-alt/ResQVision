## Decisions That Can Be Resolved from Current Requirements

The draft baseline consistently states that AI cannot verify a candidate, candidate and incident are separate concepts, assignments are independent, the backend authorizes access, evidence stays private, location retains provenance, and the prototype does not dispatch or send real emergency notifications. These can guide design and fail-closed implementation. Because the source documents remain revised drafts, this addendum records them as documented baseline constraints, not as formally approved policy.

## Proposed Amendments to Existing Entries

1. **OAD-018 clarification:** replace ambiguity around “do not aggregate acknowledgement into incident state” with: “Agency assignments and histories remain independent. Acknowledgement does not change incident state. The first assignment entering `IN_PROGRESS` may set incident `RESPONSE_ACTIVE`; this aggregate incident state does not mutate another assignment. Resolution and closure are separate incident-level decisions.”
2. **OAD-020 follow-up:** record that schema/service support does not settle role access or precision. Add a decision item for external-reported location provenance and competing claim handling.
3. **OAD-021 follow-up:** distinguish an enum value `EXPIRED` from an approved/implemented timeout transition; expiry is not currently operational.

These are proposed amendments. Preserve the existing register text until the decision owners accept the changes.

## Approval Owners and Record Keeping

Use the owner placeholders already assigned in `open_architecture_decisions.md`; do not substitute invented names. The project team owns business and role decisions. Police/Hospital representatives must approve their data/assignment expectations. Source owners and institutional privacy/data-governance representatives must approve source purpose, location precision, retention, and media deletion. Backend/security owners assess machine identity, storage transfer, transaction, and audit controls. Record the approver, date, decision status, rationale, and affected requirements in an ADR or dated OAD amendment after approval.
