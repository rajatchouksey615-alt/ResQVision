# Incident Response Workflow

**Status:** Prototype workflow template. No incident lifecycle or external integration is implemented.

Describe the incident lifecycle from candidate receipt and verification through authorized review and closure. Define each state transition, actor, permission, required data, audit event, and failure outcome before building the backend.

Prototype responses must be simulated. Do not send real alerts to police or hospitals, dispatch ambulances, or imply operational readiness. Define how unverified candidates are labeled and who may confirm or dismiss them.
